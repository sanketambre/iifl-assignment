# Loan Prepayment and Foreclosure Policy

## Definitions

**Part-prepayment** means paying an amount over and above the scheduled EMI while the
loan remains active. **Foreclosure** (also called full prepayment or preclosure) means
repaying the entire outstanding principal and closing the loan before the scheduled
end of tenure.

## Lock-in period

Foreclosure is permitted only after **12 EMIs have been paid** from the date of first
disbursement. Part-prepayment is permitted after **6 EMIs** have been paid. Requests
received before the applicable lock-in period will be rejected.

## Foreclosure charges

Charges depend on the interest rate type and the borrower category.

| Loan type | Borrower | Foreclosure charge |
|---|---|---|
| Floating rate, individual borrower | Individual | **Nil** |
| Fixed rate, individual borrower | Individual | 3% of principal outstanding |
| Any rate type | Non-individual (company, firm, LLP) | 4% of principal outstanding |

Goods and Services Tax is applicable on all charges. As required by regulation, no
foreclosure charge is levied on floating rate loans sanctioned to individual borrowers
for purposes other than business.

## Part-prepayment charges

Part-prepayment of up to **25% of the principal outstanding in a financial year** is
free of charge for all borrowers. Any amount above that limit attracts a charge of
2% on the excess amount. A minimum part-prepayment of Rs. 25,000 applies.

## How to request foreclosure

1. Raise the request through the customer portal, the mobile app, or any branch.
2. A foreclosure statement is issued within **3 working days**, valid for 15 days.
3. Pay the quoted amount by NEFT, RTGS, or demand draft. Cash is not accepted for
   foreclosure.
4. The loan is closed within **7 working days** of the payment clearing.

## Documents released after closure

The No Objection Certificate (NOC) is issued within **7 working days** of closure.
Original property documents, where held as security, are returned within
**30 calendar days** of closure from the branch where the loan was serviced.

## Effect on credit bureau reporting

Closure is reported to credit bureaus in the reporting cycle following closure.
Bureau records may take up to **45 days** to reflect the closed status.

# KYC and Account Detail Update Policy

## Periodic re-KYC

Customers must complete periodic re-KYC based on their risk category:

| Risk category | Re-KYC frequency |
|---|---|
| Low risk | Every 10 years |
| Medium risk | Every 8 years |
| High risk | Every 2 years |

Customers are notified **60 days** before re-KYC falls due. If re-KYC is not completed
by the due date, transactions on the account may be restricted until it is done.

## Accepted officially valid documents

Any one of the following is accepted as proof of identity and address: Aadhaar,
passport, voter ID, driving licence, or NREGA job card. PAN is mandatory as a separate
proof of identity for all loan accounts and cannot be substituted.

## Updating your mobile number

A mobile number change is treated as a sensitive update and cannot be completed
online end to end.

1. Submit the request through the mobile app, the customer portal, or a branch.
2. Verify with an OTP sent to the **existing registered mobile number**. If the
   existing number is no longer accessible, the update must be done in person at a
   branch with an original officially valid document.
3. The change takes effect within **2 working days**.

Only one mobile number change request is permitted in any 30 day period.

## Updating your email address

Email updates can be completed fully online. An OTP is sent to the new email address
and the change takes effect **immediately** on verification.

## Updating your address

Submit a self-attested copy of a current officially valid document showing the new
address, through the app, portal, or branch. Address updates are processed within
**7 working days**. If the document is unclear or the name does not match account
records, the request is rejected and the customer is notified with the reason.

## Updating your name or date of birth

Name and date of birth corrections require a branch visit with original supporting
documents such as a gazette notification, marriage certificate, or court order.
These requests are reviewed by the Compliance team and take up to
**15 working days**.

## Updating bank account details for disbursement or refunds

Requires a cancelled cheque or a bank statement from the last 3 months showing the
account holder name. Processed within **5 working days** after a penny-drop
verification of the account.

# EMI, Charges and Payments FAQ

## What happens if my EMI payment bounces?

A bounce (also called a dishonoured mandate or failed auto-debit) attracts a
**bounce charge of Rs. 500 per instance**, plus applicable GST. This is separate from
late payment interest.

## What is the late payment charge?

Late payment interest of **2% per month** is charged on the overdue EMI amount,
calculated from the EMI due date until the date the payment is received. Late payment
interest and the bounce charge can both apply to the same missed EMI.

## Is there a grace period?

Payments received within **3 calendar days** of the due date do not attract late
payment interest. The bounce charge still applies if an auto-debit attempt failed.

## Can I change my EMI due date?

Yes. The EMI date can be changed **once during the life of the loan**. Available due
dates are the **1st, 5th, 10th, or 15th** of the month. Submit the request through the
app or portal at least **10 days before** the next EMI date. A new NACH mandate must
be registered and the change takes effect from the following month. There is no fee
for the first change.

## How do I register or replace a NACH mandate?

A NACH mandate can be registered online using net banking or debit card
authentication, which activates within **5 working days**. Physical mandates take up
to **21 working days**. An existing mandate must be cancelled before a new one for the
same loan becomes active.

## What payment methods are accepted?

Auto-debit via NACH, UPI, net banking, debit card, and NEFT/RTGS. Cash is accepted at
branches up to **Rs. 20,000 per transaction**. Credit cards are not accepted for EMI
payment.

## When is my payment reflected in the account?

UPI and net banking payments reflect the **same working day**. NEFT and RTGS reflect
within **1 working day**. Cheque payments reflect after clearing, typically
**3 working days**.

## How do I get an EMI or interest statement?

Account statements, interest certificates, and repayment schedules can be downloaded
from the app or portal at any time, free of charge. Branch-issued physical copies
attract a fee of Rs. 200 per document.

## Who do I contact about a charge I believe is incorrect?

Raise a dispute through the app, the portal, or the grievance email address. Disputes
are acknowledged within **3 working days** and resolved within **30 days**. If you are
not satisfied with the resolution, the matter can be escalated to the Principal Nodal
Officer and thereafter to the regulatory ombudsman.
